# Stage 7.5A — Generality / Quantifier Certification

## Verdict

`GO — GENERALITY / QUANTIFIER CERTIFICATION PASS`

## Certified scope

1. The `4/27` theorem is a baseline global best-response result at maximal locations. It is not a corrected full-game SPNE threshold.
2. The asymmetric exclusion result is an existence/multiplicity result: at least two additional quality equilibria exist. It is not a complete equilibrium characterization.
3. Within the original Theorem-2 interval, the reported symmetric profile is a quality-subgame Nash equilibrium iff `4/27≤λ<2/9`.
4. Welfare dominance between the certified exclusion equilibria and the reported symmetric equilibrium may be stated as an equilibrium comparison only on `4/27≤λ<2/9`; below `4/27` the symmetric profile is not an equilibrium.
5. The `4/21` threshold is a consumer-surplus threshold for the named profile comparison only.
6. The pure-SPNE nonexistence result must be qualified as applying under the standard modern full-history SPNE interpretation.
7. The unrestricted planner benchmark may be called first best; the `(0,1)` benchmark must be called the fixed-location planner optimum.
8. Linear-transport rescaling to `λt=4/27` is exact parametric robustness. No arbitrary-transport robustness is certified.
9. Strict convexity of quality cost alone does not establish the regime-switch mechanism; no general convex-cost theorem is part of the frozen theory.

## Explicitly prohibited wording

- “the corrected equilibrium threshold is `4/27`” without the quality-subgame/maximal-location qualifier;
- “the corrected equilibrium is unique”;
- “the paper characterizes all quality equilibria”;
- “all equilibria welfare-dominate the reported symmetric outcome”;
- “the result holds for general convex quality costs”;
- “the result is robust to general transportation technologies.”

No added equilibrium-selection rule or refinement is used.
