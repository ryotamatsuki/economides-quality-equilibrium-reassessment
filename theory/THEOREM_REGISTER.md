## Prohibited upgrades

- Existence is not uniqueness.
- Additional equilibria are not a complete equilibrium correspondence.
- The `4/27` result is not a full-game SPNE threshold.
- Named-profile welfare comparisons are not all-equilibria welfare claims.
- Exact linear-transport rescaling is not robustness to arbitrary transportation technologies.
