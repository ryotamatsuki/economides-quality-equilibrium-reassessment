## What the directly inspected Bunte (1997) material establishes

Bunte is not merely adjacent literature. Chapter 3 explicitly compares his analysis with Economides (1989).

- On p. 92, Bunte says that Economides obtains maximum differentiation despite using essentially the same model and attributes the difference to Economides not imposing a stability condition on the R&D decision. Bunte states that imposing the stability condition leaves no parameter values for maximum differentiation in his Hotelling scenario.
- The same discussion distinguishes three resolutions of linear-Hotelling price instability: the d’Aspremont-Gabszewicz-Thisse quadratic-transport solution, a Hotelling scenario based on recognition of mutual dependence, and a limit-price scenario.
- On p. 106, Bunte states that he employs the same specifications as Economides and that the derivations correspond, but again excludes Economides’s maximum-differentiation parameter region for stability reasons.
- The p. 106 footnote reports that Sorenson (1995) uses the same specification as Economides for the R&D problem, reports the same stability condition as Bunte, and does not address the variety decision.

Therefore the present project must not claim:

- first discovery that Economides (1989) has a stability problem;
- first challenge to the maximum-differentiation result;
- first recognition that the quality/R&D choice needs a global stability check.

These are killed contribution claims irrespective of whether the unavailable full texts are later obtained.

## Operative prior-art conclusion after Stage 14R

Historical Stage-10R classification: `STRUCTURALLY VERY CLOSE BUT NON-ABSORBING`.

**Superseding evidence-bounded classification:** `STRUCTURALLY VERY CLOSE; EXACT OVERLAP UNRESOLVED FOR UNAVAILABLE FULL TEXTS`.

Bunte (1997) definitively absorbs the broad criticism that Economides's quality/R&D calculation requires a stability check. Sorenson, through the publisher abstract and Bunte's report, is also directly relevant to that prior stability literature. What the current paper may claim is therefore not historical priority, but the results it derives and proves itself: the piecewise maximal-location continuation of the original noncooperative price game, the associated regime-changing deviation and `4/27` condition, and explicit asymmetric exclusion quality equilibria.

No equivalent result has been identified in the materials examined. Because the full texts of Bunte (1995) and Sorenson (1995) were not independently inspected, this record does **not** establish that equivalent results are absent from those works and does **not** certify non-absorption by them. Exact overlap remains unresolved.

## Manuscript rule

The manuscript should describe the contribution as a self-contained derivation of the stated original-game results. It should not imply:

- priority over Bunte or Sorenson;
- exhaustive prior-art clearance;
- absence of an equivalent `4/27` condition or exclusion equilibrium in an unavailable full text;
- novelty of the generic methodological proposition that upstream choices can alter downstream market structure or that global best responses must be checked.

A concise admissible formulation is: “Our comparison with Sorenson (1995) is limited to the available abstract and Bunte's discussion; it does not establish the absence of equivalent results in that article. We make no priority claim for the results derived here.”
