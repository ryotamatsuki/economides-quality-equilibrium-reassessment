## Findings that remain valid

Bunte is not merely adjacent literature. Chapter 3 explicitly compares his analysis with Economides (1989).

- Bunte states that his specifications/derivations correspond to Economides but that Economides's maximum-differentiation conclusion disappears when Bunte's R&D-game stability condition is imposed.
- Bunte distinguishes alternative resolutions of linear-Hotelling price instability, including a Hotelling scenario based on recognition of mutual dependence and a limit-price scenario.
- Bunte attributes the same R&D stability condition to Sorenson (1995), while noting that Sorenson focuses on the R&D decision.

Therefore the present project must not claim:

- first discovery that Economides (1989) has a stability problem;
- first challenge to the maximum-differentiation result;
- first recognition that the quality/R&D choice requires a stability/globality check.

These firstness claims remain permanently killed.

## Stage-10R2 correction to the earlier non-absorption conclusion

The earlier version of this file classified the relationship as `STRUCTURALLY VERY CLOSE BUT NON-ABSORBING`. Stage 10R2 withdraws that certification-level wording.

The accessible Bunte (1997) material supports a useful mathematical distinction: after mapping Bunte's R&D-cost and transport parameters into the present normalization, Bunte's stated Hotelling-scenario R&D-stability cutoff maps to `theta>2/9`, whereas the present finite branch-changing deviation threshold is `theta=4/27`. The conditions are therefore not algebraically identical.

That observation is **not** sufficient to certify overall non-absorption. The full Bunte (1995) working paper and the full Sorenson (1995) article were not obtained in the Stage-10R2 lawful-access search. In particular, “no equivalent `4/27` result was found” cannot be converted into “no equivalent result exists.” Likewise, Sorenson's lack of a variety decision, as reported by Bunte, is not a sufficient non-absorption argument because the present headline propositions condition on maximal locations and concern the quality/price continuation game.
