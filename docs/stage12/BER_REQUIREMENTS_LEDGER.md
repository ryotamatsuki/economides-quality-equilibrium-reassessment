## Stage 13 working contract
1. Prepare BER **Note/Comment** source and submission package; retain the original theory source, diagrams and full proofs without alteration of theorem scope.
2. Use a named title/abstract as the BER guidelines indicate; **do not** apply the Economics Bulletin rule requiring anonymous/titleless PDF. Abstract at most 150 words pending authenticated portal check.
3. Present exact original Economides (1989) result and the counterexample/derivation bridge, then the scoped P4 complete pure-quality Nash-equilibrium correspondence. Distinguish this from a new full three-stage SPNE.
4. State publication value without historical priority claims; explicitly address why identifying local quality stationarity versus global regime-switching deviations matters to an economist beyond the specific example. No unsupported generic/welfare/policy results.
5. Current Economics Bulletin full-read comparators remain useful writing examples but are **not** evidence of BER editorial acceptance; BER-specific published exemplars need separate full-text verification.
6. Non-OA route is the provisional cost-minimizing path, not an assertion that all charges are zero. Verify any portal fees and policy/AI requirements before submission.
7. No submission, fee payment, or formal Stage12/13 GO until prior scientific gate status is resolved.
