Dear Editors of the Review of Industrial Organization,

Please consider the manuscript “Global Quality Deviations and Equilibrium Multiplicity in Spatial Competition: Revisiting Economides (1989)” for publication in the Review of Industrial Organization.

The paper revisits the three-stage location–quality–price model of Economides (1989) while retaining its original noncooperative price game. At maximal locations, it derives the price continuation over the full quality-gap domain rather than only the regular shared-market branch. This reveals a finite quality deviation that changes the downstream pricing regime: the reported symmetric quality profile fails to be a quality-subgame equilibrium for \(1/9<\lambda<4/27\), while two explicit asymmetric exclusion quality equilibria exist throughout the original parameter interval. The paper also reports the associated welfare and consumer-surplus comparisons and provides reproducible symbolic, numerical, regression, and targeted Lean verification artifacts.

The manuscript’s claims are deliberately limited to the results derived and proved in the paper; it does not claim a complete characterization of the full pure-strategy equilibrium set. The paper is not under consideration elsewhere. This research received no external funding, and I declare no competing interests.
