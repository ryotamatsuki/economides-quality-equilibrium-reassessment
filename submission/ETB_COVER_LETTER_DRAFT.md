# ETB cover letter — DRAFT / NOT SENT

Dear Editor-in-Chief,

I wish to submit for consideration a short theoretical note entitled
*Quality Differentiation and Market Exclusion: A Note on Economides (1989)*
to *Economic Theory Bulletin*, once the author's final originality and
submission declarations have been completed.

The note reassesses the quality-continuation requirement for the
symmetric, maximally differentiated candidate in a linear Hotelling
duopoly. Holding firms at opposite endpoints, I derive global price
continuation with market-sharing and exclusion branches, and a complete
pure Nash equilibrium correspondence for the reduced quality game on
the relevant parameter interval. Two asymmetric exclusion equilibria
persist throughout that interval, while a symmetric quality equilibrium
is also present at and above the threshold 4/27. The result distinguishes
an invalid proposed symmetric continuation from the absence of all pure
quality equilibrium; it does not solve the endogenous-location
subgame-perfect equilibrium problem.

Earlier literature discussed instability of quality choice in the same
research tradition, particularly Bunte (1995, 1997) and Sorenson (1995).
This note makes no claim to priority for that critique. The precise
overlap of its complete fixed-location classification with those
earlier studies remains a critical editorial issue.

A broader version, titled *Global Quality Deviations and Equilibrium
Multiplicity in Spatial Competition: Revisiting Economides (1989)*,
was rejected by the *Review of Industrial Organization* on
17 November 2026. This revised note is the intended new article unit,
not a simultaneous submission. Source materials and working versions
have been visible in the public research repository:
https://github.com/ryotamatsuki/economides-quality-equilibrium-reassessment

The work has no external funding and no relevant competing interests.
OpenAI ChatGPT materially assisted theoretical exploration, coding,
formal verification, literature discovery and drafting, as described
in the manuscript. I have reviewed the reduced-game equilibrium proof
and accept responsibility for the paper.
