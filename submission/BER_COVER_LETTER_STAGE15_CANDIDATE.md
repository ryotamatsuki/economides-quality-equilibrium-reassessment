# BER cover letter — submission candidate (NOT SIGNED / NOT SENT)

Dear Editors,

I submit for your consideration a theoretical short note entitled
*Quality Differentiation and Market Exclusion: A Note on Economides (1989)*
for the *Bulletin of Economic Research*, subject to completion of the
author's final originality and exclusive-submission declarations.

The note examines the quality-stage continuation at maximally
differentiated locations in the covered linear Hotelling model of
Economides (1989). When globally profitable, finite quality deviations
and the induced downstream price-regime switch are admitted,
the pure-strategy quality-equilibrium correspondence contains two
asymmetric exclusion profiles throughout the original parameter
interval. A symmetric equilibrium also survives exactly when the
normalized cost parameter is at least 4/27. This distinguishes
failure of the proposed symmetric continuation from the
nonexistence of all pure quality equilibria. The analysis does not
claim to construct a new full three-stage subgame-perfect equilibrium.

The manuscript acknowledges earlier research on the quality-stability
problem, including Bunte (1995, 1997) and Sorenson (1995), and
does not claim historical priority over those studies. Its value
is the explicit model-specific, necessary-and-sufficient
classification and a reproducible statement of its scope.
A broader, earlier version was considered and rejected by the
*Review of Industrial Organization* on 17 November 2026;
that rejected submission is no longer under consideration there.
Research materials and working manuscript sources have been
posted in a public GitHub repository:
https://github.com/ryotamatsuki/economides-quality-equilibrium-reassessment

The research received no external funding, and the author
declares no competing interests. OpenAI ChatGPT assisted
theoretical analysis, formal and computational checks,
literature discovery and drafting, as declared in the manuscript.
The author has reviewed the reduced-game equilibrium argument
and accepts responsibility for the paper.
