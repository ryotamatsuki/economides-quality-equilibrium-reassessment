\section{Model}

Two firms choose locations, then qualities, then prices. Consumers are uniformly distributed on $[0,1]$ with density $\mu>0$ and utility
\[
 u_i(z)=k+a_i-p_i-|z-\ell_i|.
\]
Quality cost is $c a_i^2/2$, production marginal cost is zero, and $\lambda=c/\mu$. The maintained baseline assumes complete coverage. Full prose and notation must match the canonical freeze record.
