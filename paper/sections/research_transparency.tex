\section*{Research Transparency}

\paragraph{Data and code availability.}
This article does not analyze an external empirical dataset. The symbolic and numerical verification code, deterministic figure and table generators, regression tests, and Lean formal-verification artifacts supporting the reported results are available in the public research repository at \url{https://github.com/ryotamatsuki/economides-quality-equilibrium-reassessment}.

\paragraph{Use of generative AI.}
Generative-AI tools were used during the research and manuscript-preparation process for coding assistance, symbolic and numerical checking, formalization support, literature discovery, and editorial assistance. The author retained human control over the economic model, mathematical claims, source selection, interpretation, and final manuscript, and assumes full responsibility for the work.
