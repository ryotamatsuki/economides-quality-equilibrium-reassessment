\section{Exact Rescaling with a General Linear Transportation Coefficient}\label{sec:robustness}

The baseline normalizes the linear transportation coefficient to one. To make the normalization explicit, replace \eqref{eq:utility} by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:utility-t}
 u_i(z)=k+a_i-p_i-t|z-\ell_i|,
 \qquad t>0,
\end{equation}
and define the dimensionless parameter
\begin{equation}\label{eq:theta}
 \theta\equiv \lambda t.
\end{equation}
This section concerns the exact rescaling of the frozen baseline on the analogue of the original Theorem-2 region,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:theta-domain}
 \theta\in\left(\frac19,\frac29\right).
\end{equation}
The upper restriction is economically material for the comparison below: it ensures that the deviation from the reported regular-branch quality to $1/\lambda$ crosses the exclusion boundary.

At maximal locations, the regular price continuation becomes
\[
 p_1=t+\frac{\delta}{3},
 \qquad
 p_2=t-\frac{\delta}{3},
\]
and the exclusion boundary is $|\delta|=3t$. The reported symmetric quality on the regular branch remains $A=1/(3\lambda)$, while the exclusionary quality choice remains $1/\lambda$. Their quality gap is
\[
 \frac1\lambda-A=\frac{2}{3\lambda},
\]
so the deviation reaches the exclusion branch exactly when
\[
 \frac{2}{3\lambda}\geq 3t
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \theta\leq\frac29.
\]
Within \eqref{eq:theta-domain}, repeating the global payoff comparison therefore yields
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gain-t}
 \frac{\pi_i(1/\lambda,A)-\pi_i(A,A)}{\mu}
 =\frac{4-27\theta}{18\lambda}
 =\frac{4-27\lambda t}{18\lambda}.
\end{equation}
Thus the private regime-switch threshold within this rescaled baseline is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:threshold-t}
 \theta=\lambda t=\frac4{27}.
\end{equation}
The welfare and consumer-surplus differences similarly become
\begin{align}
 \frac{W_E-W_S}{\mu}&=\frac{10-9\theta}{36\lambda},\label{eq:w-t}\\
 \frac{CS_E-CS_S}{\mu}&=\frac{21\theta-4}{12\lambda}.\label{eq:cs-t}
\end{align}
Accordingly, the consumer-surplus threshold is $\theta=4/21$. The phrase ``equilibrium welfare comparison'' applies only on the coexistence region
\[
 \theta\in\left[\frac4{27},\frac29\right),
\]
where the reported symmetric quality profile and the certified exclusion profiles are both quality-subgame equilibria.

These statements are exact parametric rescalings within the class of linear transportation costs and the stated dimensionless parameter region. They do not establish robustness to quadratic transportation costs, arbitrary transportation technologies, or arbitrary convex quality-cost functions. Those would change the theoretical object and are outside the frozen scope of the paper.
